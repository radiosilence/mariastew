- **A download moves smoothly between readings.** The bar, the bytes done, the rate and the time left are now koan-kit-web 0.11's `Transfer`, which advances them from aria2's rate between the poller's readings and stops at the total, at a rate of nought, and when the next reading does not arrive. A reading's instant changes only when aria2's bytes or rate do, so a stalled download stops rather than running on, and an unchanged row is not streamed again. Sizes are written in the kit's words throughout, in steps of 1024 to one decimal place (`12.3 MB`); activity log entries written before this change keep their `GiB` wording. The expanded row no longer repeats the bytes and time left the transfer shows.
